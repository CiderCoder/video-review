Video Analytics – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Video Analytics ("the app") is a content-research and performance dashboard operated by Misen Co., Ltd. (米森有限公司, Taiwan) for the creator account Apple Gal (蘋果妹). This policy explains what data the app accesses through the Threads, Instagram and Facebook APIs, and how it is used.

1. Data we access

  • Your own profile and posts: username, profile information, posts, replies to your posts, and performance metrics (views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, shares, follower count).
  • Public Threads content: public posts returned by keyword search, and public profile information and public posts of accounts you look up (username, biography, follower count, post text, timestamp, permalink).

The app does not access private accounts, direct messages, passwords, or payment information.

2. How we use data

  • To show you how your own content performs.
  • To research which topics public conversations are about, so you can plan content.
  • To benchmark performance against public profiles.

We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising or profiling of individuals, and do not use it to train machine-learning models. The app does not publish, reply, or delete content on your behalf.

3. Storage and retention

Access tokens and retrieved data are stored only on the operator's own computer, with access restricted to the operator. Public content retrieved through search or profile lookup is kept for no longer than 90 days and is refreshed or deleted after that. Content that has been deleted on Threads is removed when the data is next refreshed.

4. Sharing

We do not share data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Your choices and data deletion

You can disconnect the app at any time in Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions. To request deletion of any data the app holds about you, email joyc1109@yahoo.com.tw with the subject "Data deletion request". We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email.
